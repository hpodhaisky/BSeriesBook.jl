BSeriesBook.jl

Julia companion to J. C. Butcher, B-Series: Algebraic Analysis of Numerical Methods, Springer Series in Computational Mathematics 55, Springer, 2021 (2nd edition in preparation, with H. Podhaisky).

Version 0.1 — initial release, work in progress

This is the first public version (0.1.0) of the package. It accompanies a book that is still being revised, it does not yet cover all chapters, and names and interfaces may change in later 0.x versions. Comments and bug reports are very welcome on the issue tracker.

BSeriesBook.jl implements the algorithms of the book in the book's own notation, so that every example can be recomputed:

  • trees are identified by their standard number n from Algorithm 3 of the book; every tree except τ is the β-product tₙ = t_{L[n]} ∘ t_{R[n]} (see Standard numbering);
  • a B-series is a plain vector a with a[n] = a(tₙ);
  • names follow the book (σ, γ, α, β, λ̂, Φ), with ASCII aliases;
  • arithmetic is exact (Rational{BigInt}) by default;
  • algorithms, sections, equations and tables are cited with their numbers in the printed book (1st edition), e.g. "Algorithm 3", "(6.6 d)".

BSeriesBook.jl is software for teaching and research. Besides the B-series of Runge–Kutta methods, composition, substitution and modified equations, it contains material of the book that other packages do not cover, most importantly the underlying one-step method of a general linear method and the conjugacy test of Algorithm 7 (Chapter 6). The data structures of the book are also fast; see Benchmarks.

For B-series in Julia there is also the package BSeries.jl, together with RootedTrees.jl, by H. Ranocha and D. I. Ketcheson (see Ketcheson and Ranocha, Computing with B-series, ACM TOMS 2023). It covers further classes of methods (additive and partitioned Runge–Kutta, Rosenbrock, multirate methods), coloured trees, symbolic coefficients, symplecticity and energy preservation. BSeriesBook.jl is tested against it as an independent reference. Further software for B-series is listed under Literature.

Installation

julia> using Pkg

julia> Pkg.add("BSeriesBook")

Julia 1.10 or newer is required. The only run-time dependency is the standard library LinearAlgebra.

What is implemented

BookJulia

Section 2.4, Algorithm 2: unrooted and non-superfluous unrooted treescount_unrooted_trees

Section 3.5: exact solution E(t) = 1/t!exact_solution

Not yet implemented (planned for later versions): elementary differentials and numerical evaluation of B-series, symplecticity and energy preservation, G-symplectic methods of Chapter 7, the integration methods and the B-group of Chapter 4.

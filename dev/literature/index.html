Literature

The book

  • J. C. Butcher, B-Series: Algebraic Analysis of Numerical Methods, Springer Series in Computational Mathematics 55, Springer, Cham, 2021. doi:10.1007/978-3-030-70956-3. The package follows its notation, numbering and algorithms. References in the docstrings ("Book: Section 2.5, Algorithm 3", "(6.6 d)", "Table 18") use the numbers of the printed 1st edition. A 2nd edition, with H. Podhaisky, is in preparation.

Related software

  • BSeries.jl and RootedTrees.jl, H. Ranocha and D. I. Ketcheson: the reference Julia packages for B-series. They are used in the test suite of BSeriesBook.jl as an independent check.
  • D. I. Ketcheson, H. Ranocha, Computing with B-series, ACM Transactions on Mathematical Software 49 (2023), Article 16. arXiv:2111.11680.
  • Kauri (Python): rooted trees, Hopf algebras, B-series, Runge–Kutta methods, modified equations.
  • NodePy (Python), D. I. Ketcheson: analysis of Runge–Kutta and linear multistep methods, order conditions via rooted trees, stability.

Further reading

  • J. C. Butcher, Numerical Methods for Ordinary Differential Equations, 3rd edition, Wiley, Chichester, 2016.
  • P. Chartier, E. Hairer, G. Vilmart, Algebraic structures of B-series, Foundations of Computational Mathematics 10 (2010), 407–427 (substitution law, modified equations).
  • E. Hairer, C. Lubich, G. Wanner, Geometric Numerical Integration, 2nd edition, Springer, 2006.
  • E. Hairer, S. P. Nørsett, G. Wanner, Solving Ordinary Differential Equations I: Nonstiff Problems, 2nd edition, Springer, 1993.
  • U. Kirchgraber, Multistep methods are essentially one-step methods, Numerische Mathematik 48 (1986), 85–90.
  • D. Stoffer, General linear methods: connection to one step methods and invariant curves, Numerische Mathematik 64 (1993), 395–407.

# Literature

## The book

- J. C. Butcher, *B-Series: Algebraic Analysis of Numerical Methods*,
  Springer Series in Computational Mathematics 55, Springer, Cham, 2021.
  [doi:10.1007/978-3-030-70956-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-70956-3).
  The package follows its notation, numbering and algorithms. References
  in the docstrings ("Book: Section 2.5, Algorithm 3", "(6.6 d)",
  "Table 18") use the numbers of the printed 1st edition. A 2nd edition,
  with H. Podhaisky, is in preparation.

## Related software

- [BSeries.jl](https://github.com/ranocha/BSeries.jl) and
  [RootedTrees.jl](https://github.com/SciML/RootedTrees.jl),
  H. Ranocha and D. I. Ketcheson: the reference Julia packages for
  B-series. They are used in the test suite of BSeriesBook.jl as an
  independent check.
- D. I. Ketcheson, H. Ranocha, Computing with B-series, *ACM Transactions
  on Mathematical Software* 49 (2023), Article 16.
  [arXiv:2111.11680](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.11680).
- [Kauri](https://kauri.readthedocs.io/) (Python): rooted trees, Hopf
  algebras, B-series, Runge–Kutta methods, modified equations.
- [NodePy](https://nodepy.readthedocs.io/) (Python), D. I. Ketcheson:
  analysis of Runge–Kutta and linear multistep methods, order conditions
  via rooted trees, stability.
